What “cleared” means

A listing is a claim. A clip is proof.

Sellers upload the serial and shutter count at lock-in. The rider verifies both on site, on camera, before the unit is sealed.

  • Serial mismatch cancels the order at no cost to the buyer.
  • Shutter count read from the body, not from the listing.
  • Cosmetic condition photographed at the meet point.
